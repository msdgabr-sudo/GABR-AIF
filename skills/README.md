# GABR AI Skills

The master architecture currently defines 12 non-duplicative skills:

1. **Deep Research & Verification** — primary-source research, cross-checking, counter-evidence, freshness, uncertainty.
2. **Critical Reasoning** — assumptions, contradictions, bias, alternatives, edge cases, failure modes.
3. **First Principles Innovation** — useful originality from facts, constraints, assumptions, and cross-domain analogies.
4. **Planning & Problem Solving** — goals, dependencies, constraints, risks, experiments, execution plans.
5. **Math & Data Intelligence** — mathematics, statistics, finance, forecasting, anomaly detection, quantitative checks.
6. **Software & Automation Engineering** — code, APIs, n8n, debugging, architecture, testing, rollback, observability.
7. **Business & Financial Intelligence** — accounting, finance, economics, operations, strategy, scenarios, sensitivity.
8. **Human Behavior & Learning** — psychology, cognitive science, learning science, UX/HCI without manipulation.
9. **Quran & Authentic Sunnah Research** — Quran, authentic Sunnah, recognized scholarship, source verification.
10. **Security & Risk Intelligence** — privacy, permissions, prompt injection, credentials, operational risk.
11. **Communication & Knowledge Transfer** — accurate adaptation, progressive disclosure, teaching and clarity.
12. **Memory & Continuous Improvement** — structured operational memory, failures, lessons, state and verified knowledge.

## Design rule

A Skill is a capability. A Sub-agent is an independently configured specialist that may use several Skills and Tools. Do not duplicate a specialist agent merely by copying a Skill verbatim.
